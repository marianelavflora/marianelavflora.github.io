  1. Nov 2026
    HackathonLive on Celo mainnet

    Agents on Open Rails

    Hackathon by Loops House × Celo × Ripio, in the LatAm (Ripio) and Textile FX tracks. I built the AI agent and the blockchain side, Eng. Flor Gonzales did the frontend, and Eng. Leila Spini and Claudia Rivero took care of users and the pitch. Pactá: An AI agent that gets Latin American freelancers paid by milestones, across borders, without ever going through the US dollar. The freelancer describes the job in her own words and the agent turns it into milestones; the client deposits everything upfront in their Ripio stablecoin (wARS, wBRL, wMXN); on each approved delivery the agent quotes on Textile, converts onchain and pays her in her currency, with a receipt. If the client goes quiet, it reminds them on day 3 and releases the payment on day 6. It runs on Celo mainnet: a full cross-currency payment (3,000 wARS → 10.05 wBRL through Textile) and its own ERC-8004 identity (agentId 10058).
